Emergency eye care in Clear Lake

Emergency Eye Care in Clear Lake: Something wrong with your eye right now? You can be seen today

When your eye is red, painful, injured, or suddenly changed, you should not have to wait hours or guess in an emergency-room lobby. Call us for emergency eye care in Clear Lake and, in most cases, Dr. Hyder can see you the same day, right here. Pink eye, something caught in your eye, a red or sore eye, a sudden burst of new flashes or floaters: these are real eye problems, and treating them is part of the care we provide every day.

Why urgent eye care belongs with an eye doctor

A glasses counter can't help a hurting eye

When your eye hurts or changes today, a price-led eyewear chain like America's Best is built to sell frames and coupons, not to treat an eye that is scratched, infected, or suddenly seeing spots. And a general emergency room, as busy as it is, is not set up for eyes; you can wait for hours and still be handed off to someone else.

Here it works differently. You reach a real doctor right away, one who works with eyes all day long and often already knows yours. Your eye does not wait, and being seen quickly is how you protect your sight. We will also be straight with you: if what you are facing needs a surgeon or a hospital, we will tell you plainly, help you get there, and coordinate your care. We do not pretend every problem is handled here.

Why trust your eye to Dr. Hyder

Caring for Clear Lake eyes for 42 years

There is a real reason to bring an urgent eye problem here. Dr. Hyder has cared for Clear Lake and Bay Area families since 1984, 42 years, one doctor, every visit. When you come in, he can pull your history and see what actually changed, instead of meeting you cold. With a sudden eye problem, knowing what your eyes looked like before is often what makes today's answer clear. In most cases he can look at it the same day, tell you plainly what is going on, and treat it right there.
