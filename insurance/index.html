How the billing works

Two kinds of visits, two kinds of coverage

A lot of the coverage confusion comes down to one thing: an eye visit can be billed two different ways, and they do not work the same. A routine vision exam - the one for updating your glasses or contacts - is usually billed to your vision plan. A medical eye problem - dry eye, pink eye, or a diabetic or glaucoma check - is often billed to your medical plan instead. Because the two are handled differently, it is worth a quick call to confirm what your specific plan covers before your visit.

Coverage depends on your plan

We do not publish a price list, and that is not a dodge. What you pay depends on your plan and on what your visit actually involves, so a number only means something once we have looked at your benefits with you.

For anything that is self-pay or not covered, we will tell you plainly what to expect, and CareCredit financing is there if it helps you spread it out. Whenever you have a coverage or pricing question, the honest way to a real answer is a quick phone call where a person can check your benefits.
