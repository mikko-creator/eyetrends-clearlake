What we carry

Three ways a frame earns its place.

Most of what makes a pair work is not the label on the temple, it is the material, the weight, and how it is set on your face. Here is how we group the wall when we are helping someone choose.

Acetate Hand-finished, and warm on the skin Deeper colour and a substantial feel. Holds an adjustment well, and suits a face that wants some definition around the eye.

Hours and directions

The looks people ask for

Most people arrive with a look in mind.

You do not need the vocabulary to get the right pair, but it helps to know what to point at. These are the three directions we hear most in the chair, and roughly who each one tends to suit.

01 The Classics Quiet acetates and thin metals that never date. If you want a pair you stop noticing by the second week, start here.
02 Bold Statement Heavier acetate, stronger colour, a shape that reads across a room. Worth trying on even if you think it is not you, because it usually is.
03 Featherlight Titanium and rimless builds for a strong prescription or a long day. The pair people choose when their last one left marks on the nose.

Not sure which of those is you? That is what the appointment is for.

Why we finish in person

The last part of choosing a frame happens in person.

A frame that looks right on a screen can sit wrong on your face. Book your eye exam and we finish it together: the measurement, the bridge, the temple length, and how the pair sits after an hour rather than a second. Then we adjust it again whenever it needs it.

Choosing the right pair

Choosing well takes someone who knows your eyes. Here in Clear Lake since 1984, for 42 years, Dr. Jerry Hyder gets to know your face, your prescription, and how you actually live in your glasses, then helps you land on the pair that is right.
