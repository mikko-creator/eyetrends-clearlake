Kids' eyewear · Clear Lake

Kids' eyewear in Clear Lake, sized and fitted properly.

A child's frame has a harder job than an adult's. It has to survive recess and still sit correctly, because a pair that slides down the nose is a prescription the child is not actually looking through. We size, fit and re-adjust kids' eyewear in house, as often as it takes.

Flexible, bendable builds that survive real useSized to the child's face, not scaled down from an adult frameRe-adjusted free whenever they knock them out of shape

Bring them in and we will size the frame properly. Call us for pricing on kids' frames and lenses.

What makes it work

Three things we check on every child's frame.

Parents usually ask about durability first. It matters, but it is the fit that decides whether the glasses actually get worn.

Flexible builds Bends instead of snapping Memory metal and flexible nylon take the abuse that ends a rigid frame. Spring hinges give when a frame is pulled off one-handed, which is how most of them come off.
Book an eye exam
The right size Fitted to the face they have now Bridge width and temple length matter more than the label. A frame that sits right stays on, and a frame that stays on is one they will actually look through.
Book an eye exam
Adjustments Bring them back as often as you need Kids bend frames. That is expected. Come in and we will straighten them, tighten the screws and re-set the fit, no appointment needed for an adjustment.
Hours and directions

What they actually pick

Three directions, and all of them are fine.

The frame a child chooses is the frame a child wears. We steer on fit and durability, and let them steer on how it looks.

01 Everyday Play Neutral colours and tough builds for children who are hard on everything. The pair that survives the school year.
02 Bright & Bold Strong colour and a shape with some personality. Chosen far more often than parents expect, and worn far more willingly.
03 Feather-Light Flex The lightest builds we carry, for a stronger prescription or a child who says everything feels heavy on their face.
