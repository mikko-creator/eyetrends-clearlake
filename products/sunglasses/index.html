What we fit

Three ways people use a second pair.

A sunglass is a prescription device as much as a style choice. How you spend your daylight decides the lens as much as the frame does.

Polarized Cuts the glare off water and asphalt The default for driving the Gulf Freeway or spending a day on the bay. Kills reflected glare rather than just dimming everything down.

Hours and directions

The looks people ask for

Three directions, depending on the day.

Most people come in knowing roughly how they will use the pair. That is usually enough for us to narrow the wall down quickly.

01 The Icons The shapes that have outlasted every trend: aviators, wayfarers, round metals. Safe in the best sense, and they suit most faces.
02 Built for the Water Wraparound fits and heavy polarization for boating and fishing. Grippy temples that stay on when it is hot and you are moving.
03 Everyday Shade Lighter tints and gradients for the school run and the commute. Comfortable enough that you forget you put them on.

Not sure which tint suits how you actually spend your day? We will work it out at the exam.
