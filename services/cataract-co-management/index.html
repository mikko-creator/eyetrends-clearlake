The slow clouding

Vision clouds slowly, then comes the hand-off

Cataracts rarely announce themselves. Vision clouds a little at a time, headlights throw more glare on the drive home, and colors that used to be crisp start to look washed out. By the time most people mention it, they've been quietly working around it for months.

Then comes the part that's harder than it should be. At a lot of places you get a quick diagnosis, a referral slip, and a handshake - and you're left to figure out what happens before, during, and after surgery on your own. That's not care; that's a hand-off.

We built this practice for the Clear Lake and Bay Area families and adults who want the other thing: thorough, personal eye care from a doctor who takes the time to sit with you and explain it, not rush you toward the door. Something as significant as cataract surgery deserves a doctor who already knows you.

What you can count on

Three things cataract co-management gives you

01

The decision to have cataract surgery, and the lens options that come with it, get explained the way a person can actually follow - unhurried, with time to ask every question. We talk, we visit, and you leave knowing what your options mean for your day-to-day life.

03

Keep the follow-up care right here.

You don't get handed off and forgotten. Dr. Hyder coordinates the referral, stays in the loop with your surgeon, and handles the pre-op and post-op visits right here - the care around your surgery, from the first exam through recovery.

Inside co-management

What co-management actually covers

Co-management means we handle the evaluation and the before-and-after care, and partner with a trusted surgeon for the procedure itself. Here's what that looks like at Eye Trends:

  • A dilated look at the lens of the eye, so Dr. Hyder can see the cataract clearly and judge how far along it is.
  • An honest read on how much it's affecting the things that matter - reading, screens, and especially night driving.
  • A plain-language conversation about timing and lens options, so you're choosing on your terms, not on a clock.
  • A referral to a trusted cataract surgeon when it's time, with your history and measurements sent along.
  • The pre-op and post-op visits handled back here, with the doctor who already knows your eyes.

The surgery itself is performed at the surgeon's surgical center - not here. What we own is everything around it: the evaluation, the coordination, and the follow-up care that makes sure the result holds.

Common questions

Straight answers before you book

Do you perform the cataract surgery here?

No. Dr. Hyder handles the evaluation and the pre-op and post-op care, and coordinates with a trusted cataract surgeon who performs the procedure at their surgical center. The surgery is done in the right hands, and the care around it stays with us.

Is cataract care covered by insurance?

It often is. Cataract evaluation and co-management are usually billable through Medicare and medical insurance plans. Because coverage depends on your specific plan, give us a call and we'll help you check.

When is it actually time for surgery?

When the cataract is genuinely getting in the way of the things you need to do - driving at night, reading, seeing faces clearly. There's no rush and no set date; Dr. Hyder helps you weigh it and decide when the time is right for you.
