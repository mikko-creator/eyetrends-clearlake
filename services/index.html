Why your history stays put

Kids' exams, medical care, urgent visits and contacts, all handled here

At a big-box counter or a rotating group practice, you see whoever is working that day, and you tend to get moved through in about fifteen minutes by a face you may not see again next year. When your kids' exams, a medical concern, an urgent visit, and your contacts are all spread across different people, your history gets handed off every time, and you end up re-explaining yourself.

Here it works the other way. Your child's first exam, a medical concern like dry eye or glaucoma, an urgent visit for a red eye, and your contact lens fitting are all handled here in Clear Lake, by Dr. Hyder. Your history stays here, so you are not re-explaining yourself every visit. We take the time to talk and to actually look, rather than moving you through in about fifteen minutes.
