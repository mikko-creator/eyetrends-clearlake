The status quo

Marketed the surgery, then on your own for the healing

Most LASIK marketing is built to sell the procedure. You answer an ad, you meet a surgeon once at a high-volume laser center, and if the surgery happens you're often on your own afterward - driving back across Houston for every drop check, every dry-eye question, every "is this normal?" moment in those first weeks. The people making the decision barely know you, and the people you can reach afterward barely remember you.

We built this practice for the other kind of patient: Clear Lake and Bay Area families and adults who want thorough, personal eye care from a doctor who takes the time - not a sales counter with a laser attached. A decision to have surgery on your eyes deserves someone who will sit with you, tell you the truth, and be there for the healing. We don't quote you a candidacy rate we can't stand behind; we look at your actual eyes and tell you what's true for you.

What we cover

What co-management actually covers

Co-management means we handle the consultation and the care around the surgery, and partner with a trusted surgeon for the procedure itself. Here's what that looks like at Eye Trends:

  • A LASIK consultation and candidacy evaluation - an honest read on whether your eyes are suited to the procedure.
  • A referral to a trusted LASIK surgeon once you're a good candidate, with your history and measurements sent along.
  • The pre-operative measurements and prep, so the surgeon has what they need before the day.
  • A series of post-operative visits that watch your healing and manage the dryness that's common in the first weeks.

The surgery itself is performed at the surgeon's laser center - not here. What we own is everything around it: the honest evaluation, the coordination, and the follow-up care that helps the result hold. LASIK at Eye Trends is co-management only, and that's on purpose - it keeps the decision and the healing here in Clear Lake from start to finish.

Common questions

Honest answers before you book

Do you perform the LASIK surgery here?

No. We handle the consultation and all of your pre- and post-operative care, and we refer you to a trusted surgeon who performs the procedure at their laser center. The evaluation and the follow-up stay here - the surgery is done in the right hands, and the care around it stays with us. LASIK here is co-management only.

Am I even a candidate for LASIK?

Maybe, maybe not - and that's exactly what the consultation is for. Some eyes are well suited to LASIK and some aren't, and the only way to know is a real evaluation. Dr. Hyder will tell you straight, with no surgery date pushed on you before you're sure.

Is co-management covered by insurance?

The LASIK procedure itself is typically elective, while the co-management visits are often billable through your medical plan. Coverage depends on your specific plan, so give us a call and we'll help you check what applies.
